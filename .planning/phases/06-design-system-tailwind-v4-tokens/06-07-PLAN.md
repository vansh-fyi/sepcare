<objective>
Restyle Card against its two confirmed Figma nodes, resolve the 4 still-unconfirmed card-type nodes to
either a new subcomponent, a documented composition, or a duplicate (D-12's own discretion clause makes
this an expected outcome, not a shortfall), and install+restyle the shadcn `Item` row primitive that the
Home-proof Instructions list and any "instruction-row"/"list-item" card pattern will compose from.

Purpose: Card is the single most-reused surface primitive in the expanded D-12 set — six Figma nodes
reference "card types" but the "layout never changes" rule (card.DESIGN.md) means most of those are
compositions on top of one restyled primitive, not six new components. Getting this reconciliation right
here prevents both under-building (missing a genuinely distinct card shape) and over-building (six
needless component files).
Output: A Figma-verified `card.tsx` + a card.DESIGN.md with a fully resolved 6-row Card Type Map, plus a
restyled `item.tsx` for row composition.
</objective>
