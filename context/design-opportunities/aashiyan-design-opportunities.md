## Medicine Safety And Adherence

### `84:345` Fake Or Substandard Medicines

Fake or substandard medicines are common in low resource markets, and patients have no way to verify authenticity before buying. design opp. QR scratch code verification app integrated into pharmacy purchase flow, usable by low literacy users

### `84:359` Elderly Medication Adherence

Elderly Medication Adherence Elderly patients on multiple medications often mix up doses or timing leading to complications. Visual voice guided pill reminder caregiver linked dashboard for elderly with low tech literacy

## Emergency Response And Matching Systems

### `84:352` Emergency Response Delay

Emergency Response Delay there is no way to alert nearby help or hospitals fast during a medical emergency ambulances arrive late or people don't know basic first response. Crowd sourced, GPS based emergency alert app connecting bystanders, nearest hospital, and ambulance in real time

### `84:370` Blood Donation Matching

Blood Donation Matching Blood shortages happen not from lack of donors, but poor real time matching between donors, blood banks, and patients in need. Real time donor matching somethin with verified blood banks, especially for rare blood types
