## India Program-Specific Health Systems

### `93:481` Nikshay

Nikshay Tuberculosis Management System

### `93:488` IHIP

IHIP National Disease Surveillance Real-time reporting of infectious diseases. used by National Centre for Disease Control (NCDC)

### `93:453` CoWIN

CoWIN National Vaccination Platform Track vaccination records across India. Designed primarily for COVID-19. Not fully integrated into a lifelong immunization record across all vaccines.

### `93:460` National Cancer Registry Programme

National Cancer Registry Programme (NCRP) Disease Registry Collect cancer incidence data across India. Provides one of India's most reliable long-term cancer datasets. Many cases from private hospitals and rural populations remain underreported, limiting nationwide completeness.
