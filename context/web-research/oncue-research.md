# OnCue: Technical & Architectural Research Report

> **Document Context**: Technical specifications, sensory feedback mechanics, and ergonomic architecture of **OnCue**, the James Dyson Award 2025 Global Medical Winner designed for individuals with Parkinson's disease.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Overview

**OnCue** is an adaptive, smart keyboard and wearable wristband system engineered to assist individuals living with **Parkinson's disease**. Designed by Italian product designer **Alessandra Galli** at **Delft University of Technology (TU Delft)** in collaboration with ParkinsonNL, the invention was awarded Global Winner (Medical Category) in the **2025 James Dyson Award**.

## 2. Problem Statement & Biomechanical Mechanism

Parkinson's disease impairs the basal ganglia, leading to **bradykinesia** (slow movement), **tremors**, reduced tactile sensitivity, and episodes of **"freezing"** (sudden inability to initiate movement). Standard computer keyboards cause high typing frustration, mis-keys, and fatigue.

## 3. Core Technical & Design Specifications

### 3.1 Therapeutic Sensory Cueing
1. **Haptic Wristband & Keybed Feedback**:
   - Integrated vibration motors in the keybed and companion wristbands deliver subtle, rhythmic haptic pulses.
   - **Neurological Rationale**: External sensory rhythms bypass damaged internal basal ganglia pathways, helping users overcome motor freezing and maintain a steady typing cadency.
2. **AI Predictive Visual Backlighting**:
   - An onboard predictive algorithm calculates the most probable next letter in a word and illuminates that key with a distinct green glow.
   - **Neurological Rationale**: Visual target highlights reduce cognitive hesitation and spatial typing errors caused by visual-motor delays.

### 3.2 Ergonomics & Tactile Geometry
* **Split Layout**: Inspired by gaming and split ergonomic keyboards, positioning keys to minimize wrist deviation and shoulder strain.
* **Raised Keycap Borders**: Keycaps feature physical raised edges around their perimeter, providing tactile boundaries that prevent fingers from slipping onto adjacent keys during tremors.

### 3.3 Dynamic User Customization
* Features physical sliders and software software controls to adjust vibration amplitude and illumination brightness, catering to symptom fluctuations throughout the day.
