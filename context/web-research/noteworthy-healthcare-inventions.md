# Noteworthy Healthcare Inventions & Upcoming MedTech Devices

> **Document Context**: A curated, categorized directory of noteworthy healthcare inventions, point-of-care diagnostic devices, frugal medical innovations, and upcoming medtech frontiers—ranging from low-resource field tools to cutting-edge AI and wearable devices.

---

## Category 1: Frugal & Low-Resource Point-of-Care Diagnostics

Inventions designed to push lab-grade screening to frontline workers (ASHA workers, rural nurses) without requiring electricity, internet, or hospitals.

### 1. OncoALERT *(James Dyson Award 2025 Top 20)*
* **Overview**: A rapid, needle-free, paper-based nanotech strip test for early oral cancer screening.
* **Problem & Insight**: Oral cancer detection in rural settings typically requires a biopsy referral, traveling to a distant city hospital, and long lab wait times—often causing patients to miss the early intervention window.
* **Mechanism**: Uses a paper strip and nanotechnology chairside assay to detect biomarkers in saliva/mouth swabs within minutes.

### 2. Schistoscope *(James Dyson Award Finalist)*
* **Overview**: A 3D-printed, smartphone-mounted automated diagnostic microscope for waterborne parasitic infections (Schistosomiasis).
* **Mechanism**: Captures urine sample photos using smartphone optics; an onboard algorithm grades infection severity automatically.
* **Impact**: Empowers local health workers with low education to execute lab-grade parasite counting in remote villages.

### 3. DioTeX
* **Overview**: A rapid point-of-care internal hemorrhage diagnostic device.
* **Problem & Insight**: For trauma cases, traveling to a distant imaging facility takes hours. DioTeX compresses the diagnostic step so first responders can identify internal bleeding immediately in emergency transport.

### 4. Cradle VSA (Vital Signs Alert)
* **Overview**: A handheld blood pressure and heart rate monitor designed to prevent maternal mortality from pre-eclampsia, sepsis, and shock.
* **Mechanism**: Incorporates a "traffic-light" early warning system (Green = Safe, Amber = Monitor, Red = Immediate Emergency Action) that triggers alerts without needing numerical literacy.
* **Context**: Deployed across low-resource clinics in Africa and Asia.

### 5. Sight Diagnostics Olo
* **Overview**: A compact, desktop point-of-care blood analyzer that delivers a lab-grade Complete Blood Count (CBC) in 10 minutes.
* **Mechanism**: Uses computer vision and AI fluorescence imaging on a two-drop finger-prick sample, eliminating traditional venipuncture and lab transport.

### 6. Khushi Baby
* **Overview**: A low-cost NFC-enabled pendant worn by infants and mothers in rural India.
* **Mechanism**: Encrypts and stores vaccination and health history directly on an offline wearable NFC chip; community health workers read and update records by tapping a basic smartphone against the pendant.

## Category 2: Digital Stethoscopes & AI Sound Screening

Acoustic devices that convert chest auscultation into visual waveforms and real-time AI disease classifications.

### 7. Eko CORE 500™ Digital Stethoscope
* **Overview**: A next-generation digital stethoscope with a built-in full-color display showing 3-lead ECG tracings and heart rate.
* **Mechanism**: Integrates AI algorithms to detect structural heart murmurs, Atrial Fibrillation (AFib), bradycardia, and tachycardia in seconds.

### 8. AyuSynk / Ayu Devices
* **Overview**: A digital attachment (developed at IIT Bombay) that clips onto any traditional analog stethoscope.
* **Mechanism**: Amplifies heart/lung sounds up to 16x, filters out ambient noise, and converts acoustic waves into shareable audio files via Bluetooth.
* **Impact**: Allows non-specialist community workers to record cardiac sounds and forward them to distant cardiologists.

### 9. StethoMe
* **Overview**: A wireless, home-use electronic stethoscope paired with an AI respiratory analysis engine.
* **Mechanism**: Parents place the device on a child’s chest; the AI identifies and quantifies lung sounds (wheezing, coarse/fine crackles, rhonchi) to monitor asthma and respiratory infections at home.

## Category 3: Non-Invasive Sensing & Bloodless Screening

Devices that measure biological markers and physiological states without needles or invasive blood draws.

### 10. AnemoCheck (Mobile & Home)
* **Overview**: Non-invasive and minimal-bleed anemia screening tools developed by Sanguina.
* **Mechanism**:
  * **AnemoCheck Mobile**: Estimates hemoglobin levels non-invasively using a smartphone camera photo of the user's fingernail bed.
  * **AnemoCheck Home**: A paper-based colorimetric assay using a single micro-drop of blood to determine hemoglobin levels without electronics.

### 11. BEMPU TempWatch
* **Overview**: A continuous temperature-monitoring silicone bracelet for newborns.
* **Mechanism**: Features a single thermistor, a microcontroller, an LED, and an audio alert. Flashes a light and sounds an alarm when a baby's temperature drops below 36.5°C (hypothermia).
* **Impact**: Prevents newborn deaths in stretched hospital wards and home settings where parents may lack health literacy.

### 12. Athena *(James Dyson Medical Winner 2024)*
* **Overview**: A portable, battery-powered scalp-cooling headpiece designed to prevent chemotherapy-induced alopecia (hair loss).
* **Mechanism**: Uses a thermoelectric cooling system to circulate cold water around the scalp outside clinical settings, offering an affordable alternative to heavy hospital-bound scalp coolers.

## Category 4: Wearable Vital Sign Monitors & Continuous Remote Sensing

Patches and wearables that monitor patients continuously in hospitals, homes, and off-grid wards.

### 13. Neopenda neoGuard™
* **Overview**: A wireless multi-patient wearable sensor band designed for infants and adults in resource-constrained hospital wards.
* **Mechanism**: Continuously measures pulse rate, respiratory rate, blood oxygen (SpO2), and skin temperature, broadcasting data wirelessly to a central nurse tablet.

### 14. BioIntelliSense BioButton®
* **Overview**: A medical-grade continuous wearable patch worn on the upper chest.
* **Mechanism**: Passively monitors over 20 physiological parameters (heart rate at rest, respiratory rate, temperature, gait, body position) for up to 30 days post-discharge.

### 15. AliveCor KardiaMobile 6L
* **Overview**: A pocket-sized 6-lead personal ECG device.
* **Mechanism**: Users touch electrodes on the device with their fingers and knee/ankle to record 6-lead ECG data on a smartphone, detecting AFib, PVCs, sinus rhythm, and bradycardia.

### 16. Dexcom Stelo / Abbott FreeStyle Libre 3
* **Overview**: Continuous Glucose Monitoring (CGM) wearable micro-patches.
* **Mechanism**: Applies a tiny subcutaneous sensor that transmits real-time glucose trends every minute to a smartphone without fingerstick calibration.

## Category 5: Assistive Tech, Brain-Computer Interfaces & Upcoming MedTech

Frontier technology shaping neurological care, assistive devices, and autonomous AI triage.

### 17. OnCue *(James Dyson Medical Winner 2025)*
* **Overview**: A smart assistive keyboard built for individuals living with Parkinson's disease.
* **Mechanism**: Combines haptic feedback, tactile prompts, and rhythm cues to reduce typing errors and overcome motor "freezing" episodes.

### 18. Neuralink N1 & Synchron Switch (BCI)
* **Overview**: Implantable Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI).
* **Mechanism**: Decodes neural signals directly from the motor cortex (Neuralink via micro-threads; Synchron via endovascular stentrode) to allow paralyzed patients to control digital interfaces, robotic limbs, and communication apps with thought.

### 19. Skin Analytics (DERM AI)
* **Overview**: Autonomous AI software for skin cancer screening in primary care settings.
* **Mechanism**: Analyzes dermoscopic images of skin lesions, classifying melanoma, basal cell carcinoma, and squamous cell carcinoma with specialist-level accuracy.
