# SDG 11 Details: Sustainable Cities and Communities

Source: FigJam nodes `2:676`, `2:679`, `2:699` (`Segue 3.0 Brainstorming`)

---

## Goal

### `2:676` Goal Definition

Make cities inclusive, safe, resilient, and sustainable. By 2050, ~70% of people will live in cities.

---

## Major Targets

### `2:679` Key Targets

| Target | Focus Areas |
| --- | --- |
| **11.1 Affordable Housing** | Safe housing, basic services — 1.12B people live in slums/informal settlements globally (2022, up 130M since 2015), 24.8% of urban population (UN-Habitat 2024/2025); housing financialization pricing out residents in NYC, London, Berlin |
| **11.2 Sustainable Transport** | Metro, buses, cycling, walking — NYC's Jan 2025 congestion pricing ($9 toll, ~$1B/yr to transit), modeled on London/Stockholm; BRT expansion (TransJakarta, Bogotá TransMilenio); e-bike/scooter micromobility |
| **11.3 Urban Planning** | Smart cities, mixed land use — ~500 cities now use urban digital twins (2025): Singapore's Virtual Singapore, Barcelona's supercomputer-powered planning platform |
| **11.4 Cultural Heritage** | Historic buildings, museums, monuments — climate-driven heritage loss accelerating (heat/flood damage); UNESCO/ICOMOS pushing climate-adaptive conservation funding |
| **11.5 Disaster Resilience** | Floods, earthquakes, cyclones, heat waves — China's Sponge Cities program (30 pilot cities: permeable pavement, rain gardens, wetlands) cuts flood costs, though 2025 research flags limits under extreme rainfall |
| **11.6 Environmental Impact** | Waste, air pollution, sewage, recycling — PM2.5 remains leading urban environmental health risk; low-emission zones (London ULEZ) and circular-economy waste programs expanding |
| **11.7 Green Spaces** | Parks, playgrounds, urban forests — equity gap in green-space access widening in dense/informal areas; urban heat island mapping now driving mandated tree-canopy targets |
| **11.A Urban-Rural Links** | Regional development, shared infrastructure — regional/megaregion planning growing amid rural-to-urban climate migration |
| **11.b Disaster Risk Reduction Strategies** | Sendai Framework-aligned local DRR strategies; parametric/climate-risk insurance for municipalities |
| **11.d Slum Upgrading & Tenure Security** | UN-Habitat Participatory Slum Upgrading Programme: Mtwapa, Kenya (7,000 households); Mukuru, Nairobi (100,000 households); Mumbai's SPARC/SDI-backed community-led upgrading |
| **NEW — Housing Affordability & Financialization** | Rent caps, social housing expansion, community land trusts responding to rent inflation in major global cities |
