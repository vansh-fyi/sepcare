# SDG 13 Details: Climate Action

Source: FigJam nodes `2:703`, `2:706`, `2:725` (`Segue 3.0 Brainstorming`)

---

## Goal

### `2:703` Goal Definition

Take urgent action to combat climate change and its impacts — driven by fossil fuels, deforestation, and rising greenhouse gases.

---

## Major Targets

### `2:706` Key Targets

| Target | Focus Areas |
| --- | --- |
| **13.1 Resilience** | Disaster preparedness — floods, cyclones, heat, drought; COP30's Belém decision (2025) sets a trajectory to triple adaptation finance by 2035 (indicator 13.1.1 tracks deaths/missing/affected per 100k) |
| **13.2 Climate Policies** | Energy, transport, agriculture, industry — NDCs under the Paris Agreement; 2025 was the deadline for updated NDCs ahead of COP30, but most fell short of 1.5°C alignment (UNFCCC NDC Synthesis Report) |
| **13.3 Education & Awareness** | Climate science, sustainable lifestyles — Action for Climate Empowerment (ACE) programs; growing role of climate litigation (2024 ICJ advisory opinion, youth-led suits) as accountability lever |
| **13.A Climate Finance** | Renewable energy funding, adaptation projects — COP29's New Collective Quantified Goal: $300bn/yr by 2035 within a broader $1.3T/yr 'Baku to Belém Roadmap'; prior $100bn pledge met late and inconsistently |
| **13.B Vulnerable Countries** | Climate planning support for LDCs/SIDS — Loss and Damage Fund (agreed COP27, operational 2025) has $741M pledged from 27 donors; Barbados Implementation Modalities allocate $250M (2025-26), 50% to SIDS/LDCs |
| **13.c Carbon Removal & CCUS** | Direct air capture scaling: Climeworks Mammoth (Iceland), 1PointFive's Stratos (Texas, up to 2 Mt/yr), Heirloom (Louisiana, 320,000 t/yr) — still <0.01% of needed 2050 removal capacity (IEA) |
| **13.d Methane & Short-Lived Pollutants** | Global Methane Pledge (150+ countries) targets 30% cut by 2030 vs 2020; satellite methane detection (MethaneSAT, GHGSat) now names individual leak sources for enforcement |
| **13.e Just Transition & Green Jobs** | Coal-to-clean workforce transition funds (e.g. South Africa's JETP, $9.3bn); IEA estimates clean energy added ~35M jobs globally in 2024 |

## Key Indicators

### `2:725` Key Indicators

- Greenhouse gas emissions
- National climate adaptation plans (13.b.1)
- Renewable energy adoption
- Disaster-related economic losses
- Climate education initiatives
- Global mean temperature anomaly vs pre-industrial (~1.5°C, WMO 2025)
- Climate finance mobilized, USD/year (13.a.1)
- Countries with disaster risk reduction strategies (13.1.2/Sendai)
- Carbon price coverage (% global emissions)
- Global CO2 removal capacity, Mt/yr (DAC+CCUS)
- Methane emissions vs Global Methane Pledge baseline
- Loss and Damage Fund disbursements (USD, by recipient)
