# Clinical card patterns

These patterns connect shared controls to the current clinical compositions. The same exports render in the component documentation and the home example.

StatusCard accepts a status, title, description, and optional action. It owns the icon treatment, row spacing, and 16px status title. All status tones use a consistent 64px square soft rounded tile, vertically centered beside freely wrapping title and description text. Safe status renders it with the shared six-second PulseWave around a stationary Tabler smiling face, with no filled center circle. Caution uses a straight-mouth face and critical an unhappy squinting face inside faster rings (2.5s and 1.2s). All three consume PulseWave with emoji enabled, lg size fitted to the tile, and tone set from status. DeviceCard accepts a name, identifier, battery level, and optional trailing action. It composes BatteryIndicator, with a non-wrapping percentage and flexible Progress bar. `connected` defaults to true and always displays Connected or Disconnected as text. Medium and low battery messages supplement that label; false takes precedence and labels battery as last known. The clinical example places this card in Settings, not Vitals. DeviceTileRow owns the shared device-state colors.

VitalCard accepts a metric, value, unit, optional trend data, and optional clinical status. The metric selects the existing gradient and icon. Safe retains the metric gradient; caution uses a yellow-400–600 gradient and critical uses a pink-200–300 gradient. Safe filled cards keep white foreground content; caution and critical cards use dark-neutral foregrounds on their yellow and pink gradients. Keep the numeric value above the optional sparkline. trendHeight controls its height in pixels (26px default). HomeVitals composes these cards as a large Pulse trend beside stacked Temperature and Activity summaries; its geometry and 88px featured trend are shared by documentation and all Home screens. From 768px, the three cards sit side by side at equal widths. Card surfaces use semantic 1px strokes and crisp shared shadows so they remain distinct on white.

InstructionCard composes Card and Item. The icon sits in a rounded square neutral tile with both dimensions matching the title and description height. ContentTileRow owns content sizing for DeviceCard and InstructionCard; StatusCard supplies a stable 64px tile to avoid resizing as messages wrap. No minimum height forces a short card taller; the glyph scales with the tile. The icon, title, and description share one spacing rule. It can be stacked without a second card wrapper or another layer of padding.

The patterns do not calculate health status or invent clinical advice. Callers supply the clinical copy and readings. Documentation labels its values as sample data.

## Consistent example scenarios

The Safe / Caution / Critical preview control applies across Home, Vitals, Stats, and Settings without resetting navigation. Fixtures in src/lib/examples/clinical-scenarios.ts supply per-vital states: safe detail rows and Stats graphs are all green, while Home VitalCards retain their three distinct metric gradients; caution uses yellow thermoregulation and cardiac autonomic, critical activity, and three safe vitals; critical marks every vital critical, using pink fills on Home. Stats graphs, icons, and values use the same per-vital states as Vitals. Infant headings and descriptions follow the overall scenario. These are illustrative combinations, not rules for computing infant risk.

Device fixtures retain 90%, 40%, and 15% for device-detail examples. Device identity, battery, and connection labels are omitted from the app header. Clinical state never computes battery or connectivity in shared components.

VitalDetailCard accepts optional status (safe/caution/critical), which controls the icon, value, and graph lines together. Explicit status overrides legacy critical. If neither is supplied, the header defaults green and series retain supplied colors. VitalCard retains each metric gradient for safe or omitted status. Caution uses yellow and critical filled VitalCards use pink. Safe VitalCards use white foregrounds on their metric gradients. Caution uses a yellow-400–600 gradient over its yellow-600 base and critical uses a pink-200–300 gradient over its pink-300 base; both use --color-vital-alert-text for dark-neutral labels, icons, sparklines, and values. Activity text uses the same font-heading text-2xl font-bold as numeric readings.


## Shared surface depth

Caution VitalCards use --gradient-vital-caution (yellow-400/500/600), critical uses --gradient-vital-critical (pink-200/300), and both retain dark-neutral foregrounds. Safe cards retain the established per-metric gradients. Other cards inherit --gradient-surface; tile backgrounds inherit --gradient-status-sheen.

## Connection and safe signifiers

DeviceCard and DeviceDetails share DeviceConnectionLabel: an sm non-emoji PulseWave beside Connected, or a static neutral pulse beside Disconnected. Connectivity is independent of battery and infant risk. Healthy device tiles and safe VitalDetailCard icon tiles (Vitals and Stats) use --gradient-signifier-safe with --color-signifier-safe-fill and white --color-signifier-safe-on-fill. The gradient runs from a green-700/800 blend through green-800 to green-900. Chart lines and values keep their existing semantic colors. Clinical badges use lighter 100–200 tonal gradients with dark text, retaining their borderless 8px radius and independent showIcon option; Vitals badges remain text-only. Other tile states retain their established caution, critical, and neutral treatments.
