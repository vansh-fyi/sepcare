Section 01

Foundations & Core Ethos

“The interface should stay calm. Colour should appear only when information requires attention.”

70% Neutral Surfaces

White (`#FFFFFF`) cards float on soft off-white canvas (`#F7F9FC`). High contrast dark navy typography (`#182235`) ensures effortless readability in dimly lit nurseries.

20% Atmospheric Pastels

Gentle pale blue (`#EEF5FC`), soft lavender (`#F3F0FA`), and warm peach (`#FCF4EA`) create a tranquil atmosphere that reduces caregiver anxiety.

10% Interaction & Status

Interaction Blue (`#6F9FD5`) is reserved solely for actions. Green, yellow, and red are reserved strictly for health telemetry triage.

Section 14

Design System Governance

Strict rules of engagement to preserve clinical dignity, parent calm, and accessibility.

✓ DO (Prescribed Practices) ✕ DO NOT (Strictly Forbidden)
Use soft pastel atmospheres and clean whitespace (70/20/10 balance). Do NOT use neon colors, rainbow dashboards, or heavily saturated gradients.
Use Primary Blue (#6F9FD5) solely for interactive controls. Do NOT use Blue to represent health or safe physiological condition.
Communicate status multi-modally: ICON + TEXT + COLOR. Do NOT rely on color alone (e.g. a standalone green dot without text).
Display physiological metrics in large, tabular figures (98 bpm). Do NOT bury vitals in narrative prose sentences.
Maintain identical Status Card layout structure across all 3 triage states. Do NOT invent completely different card shapes or component hierarchies between states.
Reserve red (#D95757) strictly for urgent sepsis triage & clinical escalation. Do NOT make ordinary secondary actions or destructive buttons red.
