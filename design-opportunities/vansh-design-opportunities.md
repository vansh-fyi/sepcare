# Vansh Design Opportunities

Source: FigJam section `1:83` (`Design Opportunities`)

Author in FigJam: `Vansh Grover`

## Local Emergency Signaling

### `1:88` Pull Down Emergency Levers

Pull Down Emergency Levers in localities/homes:  Tiered urgency, not just binary two colored pull-cords (yellow = "check on us when you can," red = "come now")

## Door Tags And Household Status

### `1:92` Health Worker Signage Tags

Designing Signage tags which healthworkers can put outside homes: a health worker sets the door tag after a visit ("visited, all clear, next check in 2 weeks") so the next worker on rotation, or a family member, doesn't have to ask. A shared record between household and visitor, never digital.

### `1:97` Visible All-Clear State

Make the "all clear" state visible too, not just alarms most safety systems only signal problems. A quiet green tag meaning "checked, fine, nothing needed" lets health workers skip houses fast on a long route its own efficiency win.

## Cultural And Local Traditions

### `56:216` Local Traditions As Care Signals

Using Local Traditions (Eg: mauli on the wrist of pregnant women) and bending it to communicate conditions where people are prepared for support for maternal, elderly and disabled care.
